Sarai and Gateway, Nurmahal,Punjab

Ancient Site and Buddhist Stupa, Sanghol, Punjab

An important archaeological site featuring ancient Buddhist remains and a historic stupa reflecting Punjab’s rich Gandharan-era heritage.
