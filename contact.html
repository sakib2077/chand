About Chandigarh Circle

The Chandigarh Circle of ASI protects and preserves the region’s most important archaeological, architectural, and historical sites. The circle oversees monuments, museums, conservation work and public interpretation across Chandigarh and its neighbouring heritage landscapes.


Its work includes the scientific conservation of historic structures, protection of archaeological remains, maintenance of protected monuments, and documentation of sites with significant cultural value. Through regular conservation initiatives and site management, the circle helps safeguard the region’s heritage for future generations.

Historical and Cultural Significance

Chandigarh and its neighbouring regions represent a distinctive combination of ancient heritage and modern architectural history. The area contains important archaeological remains as well as historically significant buildings and cultural landscapes. The Chandigarh Circle works to ensure that these resources are properly documented, protected, and maintained.


Conservation and Preservation

A major responsibility of the Chandigarh Circle is the scientific conservation of protected monuments and heritage structures. Conservation activities may include structural repairs, restoration of architectural elements, surface treatment, preservation of historic materials, and regular maintenance. These measures help protect monuments from natural deterioration, environmental conditions, and human impact.

Archaeological Survey of India

Chandigarh Circle preserves the material heritage of the region through conservation, research, documentation, and public engagement.
